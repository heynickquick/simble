# Simble — Phone Runbook for the Friend

**What is this?** A few phones that send text messages for Simble. They just need to stay plugged in and on WiFi. You don't need to do anything technical after the initial setup.

---

## One-time setup (about 10 minutes per phone)

You'll have 2 or 3 phones. Do this for **each one**.

1. **Pick a spot** with good WiFi signal — a shelf or table near your router is best. The phones need a stable internet connection.
2. **Plug the phone charger** into the wall (or into the power strip / USB hub if Nick sent one).
3. **Plug the phone** into its charger with the cable. Use the cable Nick sent — leave the original in the box.
4. **Make sure WiFi is connected.** Open Settings → WiFi and connect to your home network. The phone should remember this and reconnect automatically forever after.
5. **Confirm the Simble Gateway app is running.** You should see a small notification on the lock screen that says **"Simble Gateway"** with a little phone icon. If you see it — good, nothing to do.
6. **Leave the phone ON, plugged in, screen-off.** The dark screen is fine — it's still working. Don't tap the notification.

That's it. Repeat for each phone.

---

## Optional: power backup

If Nick sent you a UPS battery backup (small white box), plug it in first, then plug the phone chargers into it. This keeps the phones running for ~30 minutes during a power outage. The UPS will beep every few seconds when on battery — that's normal, it stops when power returns.

If you have a USB hub (small black or white box with multiple USB ports), plug it into the wall (or UPS), then plug all the phones into it. Fewer cables to the wall.

---

## What the screens will look like

Each phone will show:

- A persistent notification on the lock screen: **"Simble Gateway"** with a small phone icon
- A dark/locked screen most of the time
- The screen may briefly light up when sending an SMS (this is normal — it's the system sending the message)

If you see the notification, **do not tap or swipe it**. Tapping the notification can stop the gateway and Nick will have to fix it remotely.

---

## What you DON'T need to do

- Don't open the Simble Gateway app
- Don't tap the notification
- Don't update the apps (Nick will tell you when)
- Don't change the WiFi network
- Don't move the phones

The system runs itself. As long as the phones are plugged in and on WiFi, they keep working.

---

## What you SHOULD do

### If the UPS beeps

- **Beeping every few seconds** = power is out, UPS is on battery. Phones will keep running for about 30 minutes. Once power comes back, the beeping stops on its own.
- **Long solid beep** = UPS battery is very low. Plug the UPS into the wall immediately if it came unplugged.

### If a phone reboots

Totally normal. The phone will:
1. Show the home screen / lock screen
2. Reconnect to WiFi (within 30 seconds usually)
3. Re-start the Simble Gateway app automatically (within 1-2 minutes)
4. Show the "Simble Gateway" notification again

No action needed unless it stays at the lock screen without the notification for more than 5 minutes.

### If WiFi goes out

Nothing to do. Phones will reconnect automatically when WiFi comes back. If a phone is still "off" (no notification on the lock screen) more than an hour after WiFi is restored, text Nick.

### If a phone shows a low battery warning

Ignore it. The phone is plugged in. The warning is about the internal battery, which is fine to be at any level while charging.

## If Nick asks you to do something

He'll tell you. Usually it's one of:

- **"Reboot the phones"** — Hold the power button on the phone for 5 seconds, wait 10 seconds, hold power again. Do this for all phones.
- **"Reconnect the WiFi"** — Open Settings → WiFi → tap your network → Forget → tap it again → enter the password (it's on the router sticker, or text Nick).

---

## What NEVER to do

- Don't unplug the phones (the battery backup is fine to use, that's its job)
- Don't factory reset the phones
- Don't uninstall the Simble Gateway app
- Don't change the WiFi password
- Don't move the phones to a different room (the WiFi signal might be weaker there)
- Don't put the phones in a drawer or cover them with anything (they need airflow to stay cool)

## Quick troubleshooting

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Lock screen shows "Simble Gateway" notification | Working normally | Nothing |
| Lock screen has no notification | Gateway app stopped | Text Nick |
| Phone is at the home screen (not lock screen) | It rebooted | Wait 5 min for it to come back, then check the lock screen |
| UPS is beeping slowly | Power out, on battery | Wait for power to return, beeping stops on its own |
| UPS is beeping solid | Battery very low | Check that UPS is plugged into wall |
| Phone shows "No SIM" or "No service" | SIM card issue | Text Nick — don't try to open the SIM tray |
| Phone screen is black, won't turn on | Battery fully dead | Unplug and replug the charger cable, wait 30 seconds |
